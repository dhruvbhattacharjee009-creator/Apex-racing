REGULATION TIMELINE

2023GROUND EFFECT

Teams developed the second year of the ground-effect regulations, with continued work on floor performance, ride control and aerodynamic efficiency.

2024REFINEMENT

Continued development of aerodynamic platforms, sidepod concepts, floor performance and cooling solutions.

2025FINAL ERA

The final season of this generation of ground-effect cars. Teams continued refining their existing platforms while preparing for the next regulations.

2026NEW GENERATION

Revised chassis and power-unit regulations, active aerodynamic systems and a new approach to energy deployment and efficiency.
